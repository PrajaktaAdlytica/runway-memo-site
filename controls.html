FILE 08 / THE PRINTED MEMORead the file ↓
01 / HUMAN AUTHORITY

Suggested, reviewed and completed are different states.

Imported data is not reconciled evidence. A drafted scenario is not an accountant approval. A correction is not an owner decision. The demo’s completed review states describe a synthetic workflow, never a completed payment.

  1. Reviewer override

    The simulated accountant can revise an opening-cash assumption with a reason. The original source remains visible and review is reopened.

  2. Owner authority

    The owner reviews the corrected memo. Any action outside the preview is separate and subject to the business’s own controls.

02 / UNCERTAINTY

An overdue invoice is not available cash.

AR-205 is billed from Elmfield Repairs to Brightwater Housing Ltd. Its expected receipt date is an assumption. The delayed branch retains both receipt and no-receipt outcomes.

Illustrative scenario range, not a probability. No guaranteed runway, savings, compliance, eligibility, payout or outcomes.

03 / DATA & EXECUTION

An isolated preview, with no financial connections.

The website has no real file upload, payment checkout, bank connection or accounting integration. Send transfer and Post entry controls are disabled. Only a labelled synthetic text report can be downloaded locally.

  1. Preview handling

    Form values and edited assumptions remain in page memory. There is no persistent browser storage or third-party analytics collection implemented.

  2. Production decisions remain open

    Access controls, retention, hosting, legal terms and data-protection arrangements require real implementation and review before sensitive data is accepted. No certification or security guarantee is claimed.

04 / PROFESSIONAL REVIEW

Liability cannot be designed away.

Incomplete books and incorrect assumptions can change a recommendation. The concept does not replace professional judgement. Pilot permissions, scope, advisory responsibilities and legal wording are informational and require appropriate professional/counsel review.

THE NEXT HANDOVER

Bring the owner and accountant.

A bounded pilot would need both people, permissioned records and a purchase decision. This preview takes no payment or sensitive records.
