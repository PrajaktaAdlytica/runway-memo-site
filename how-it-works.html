01 / THE REVIEW LOOP

Three steps. Then the owner decides.

Each handover has an explicit responsibility. Missing or unreliable records remain exceptions, not silently repaired facts.

  1. Import and reconcile

    Bring permissioned accounting, bank, receivables and payroll CSVs together. Resolve gaps before drafting a recommendation.

  2. Draft two scenarios

    Compare payment timing against the same records. Keep receipt assumptions and unpaid bills visible.

  3. Accountant reviews and corrects

    Your external accountant or fractional CFO checks the evidence and records amendments in the memo.

  4. Owner reviews and decides

    The owner considers the reviewed memo and retains authority. No payment or accounting entry is executed here.

02 / THE SOURCE PACKET

Permission first. Provenance throughout.

The proposed first pilot uses permissioned exports for two clients. This website accepts no financial files and has no live bank, payroll or accounting connections.

  1. Accounting CSV

    Check the books and recorded obligations against the relevant balances. Log differences rather than assuming the ledger is current.

  2. Bank CSV

    Establish opening cash with a dated source. Review any unmatched movements with the accountant.

  3. Receivables CSV

    Keep invoice direction, customer, expected date and collection uncertainty visible. A due date does not guarantee receipt.

  4. Payroll CSV

    Identify payroll commitments and due dates before comparing discretionary payment timing.

03 / THE AMENDMENT

A correction has a before and an after.

The synthetic draft for AR-205 uses 24 Oct 2026. The accountant corrects the expectation to 27 Oct 2026, while retaining “not guaranteed”.

The demo’s simulated reviewer can amend opening cash with a reason. Both paths recompute and earlier review is invalidated. This is not an accounting posting.

04 / THE HANDOVER

A report is the end of this tool’s authority.

The owner can inspect the reviewed memo and its traceable figures. Any payment, accounting change or external sharing happens separately under the business’s existing controls.

  1. Local illustrative export

    The demo can download a synthetic text report. Its labels, source values, assumptions and correction history remain attached.

  2. Held means unpaid

    The supplier remains an obligation in the hold scenario. The display does not mark it settled or approved for transfer.
