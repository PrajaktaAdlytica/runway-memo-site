09 / THE BOUNDARIESCONCEPT PREVIEW

Rules of
this memo.

A clear recommendation needs
equally clear limits.

  1. 01

    Human review before anything leaves the memo.

    The accountant reviews the assumptions. The owner retains the decision.

  2. 02

    Every figure stays linked to its source.

    If records do not reconcile, the exception stays visible for review.

  3. 03

    No transfers. No accounting entries.

    This is an advisory workflow. It has no authority to move funds or post to your books.

  4. 04

    An assumption is labelled as an assumption.

    An expected receipt is never treated as guaranteed cash.

Incomplete books and mistaken assumptions can change a recommendation. This concept does not replace professional judgement or resolve advisory liability.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you reply.

How is this different from forecasting tools?+

RunwayMemo explores a narrow weekly decision memo with source evidence, timing scenarios and an accountant amendment trail. Forecasting software and spreadsheets may already serve parts of that job. Whether reviewed memos add value is a pilot question, not a proven claim.

What data would a pilot use?+

Permissioned accounting, bank, receivables and payroll CSVs for a small, agreed scope. The initial research plan is two clients with their accountants. This preview uses fictional records only; there are no live integrations or file uploads.

Why are there no automatic transfers?+

A memo helps a person review a decision. RunwayMemo does not execute transfers or post accounting entries. Any real-world action takes place separately, under the owner's existing controls.

What if the books do not reconcile?+

The discrepancy needs accountant review before a recommendation can be relied on. If the accountant has to rebuild every input each week, that is a reason to stop or change the pilot.

Who needs to join the pilot?+

The business owner and the supervising external accountant or fractional CFO. They agree the scope and co-sign a paid pilot. Accountant and bookkeeper co-delivery is a proposed channel; no partnership is claimed.

Is RunwayMemo available to buy?+

This is a concept preview, not an operating product. Modules and pricing are hypotheses. The request form demonstrates a consented request; it does not enrol you, send your details or collect payment.

Privacy & deletion for this concept preview +

Form details remain in this page’s memory. No form endpoint, persistent browser storage or third-party analytics is connected. Reload the page or use “Clear my details” after preparing a request to remove the entered values.

Interaction events are logged locally with allowed role, module and campaign tags only. They contain no name, email, company or financial records.
