01 / THE JOB

From scattered records to a specific question.

Accounting, bank, receivables and payroll records tell parts of the story. A cash-constrained owner still needs to discuss which bill to pay, which receipt to question and what payroll leaves available.

  1. Bring the evidence

    Link each figure to its source file, record ID, date and assumptions. Reconcile the opening position before drawing conclusions.

  2. Compare timing

    Draft two alternatives from the same inputs. Keep uncertain receipts separate from cash already available.

  3. Leave a review trail

    The accountant corrects assumptions. The owner reviews the memo and decides separately what to do.

03 / EVIDENCE, NOT A CLAIM

Try a decision before trusting a promise.

The clickable demo uses Elmfield Repairs, a fictional services firm. It compares $2,000 versus $14,000 Friday cash, and shows why the later receivable belongs outside both Friday balances.

  1. A visible exception

    A proposed guaranteed receipt is rejected. The overdue branch remains blocked instead of quietly treating cash as received.

  2. A reversible proposal

    Changing an assumption recalculates both scenarios and reopens review. Source records remain visible and unchanged.

  3. No outcome guarantee

    There are no claimed savings, live customers, certifications or production integrations. Whether reviewed decisions add value is still a pilot question.
