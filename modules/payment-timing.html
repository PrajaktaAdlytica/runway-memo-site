01 / THE JOB

Two source-linked timing scenarios

Scenario A: $32,000 − $12,000 − $18,000 = $2,000. Scenario B: $32,000 − $18,000 = $14,000; supplier remains unpaid.

DEMO · SYNTHETIC DATA. No measured customer outcome.

02 / INPUTS & RESPONSIBILITY

The owner brings the question. The accountant reviews the evidence.

Opening cash, payroll amount and due date, the supplier bill and the expected receivable with its source invoice.

  1. 01 / Prepare

    Reconcile the starting cash and commitments.

  2. 02 / Compare

    Draft supplier-before-payroll and supplier-held alternatives.

  3. 03 / Review

    Compare delayed-receipt sensitivity with assumptions visible.

  4. 04 / Hand over

    Record accountant corrections and obtain owner review.

03 / THE EXCEPTION

What must stay visible.

Holding the bill changes timing; it does not cancel the obligation or confirm supplier agreement. The owner must consider consequences outside the demo.

Separate advisory workflow. No transfers, postings, automatic messages or system writes.

04 / THE PILOT TEST

Prove this job before bundling it.

Record whether the reviewed timing choice changes a real recurring decision, whether the cash forecast reconciles and whether both parties renew.

  1. Bounded scope

    Select this module for 4 weekly cycles, with client permission and an owner–accountant co-signed pilot.

  2. Hypothetical pricing

    Hypothetical test pricing: $300–$800/month per business plus $500 setup. Not a market benchmark.
