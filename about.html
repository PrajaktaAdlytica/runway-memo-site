ABOUT RUNWAYMEMO

Make the reasoning visible.

RunwayMemo is a research-stage concept for a weekly cash decision memo reviewed by an external accountant. The printed memo is a working document: something to inspect, correct and hand over.

01 / VISION

An evidence-linked decision, not an opaque action.

Make the weekly accountant-reviewed cash decision loop reviewable. Keep human owners in authority and allow proposed changes to be revised before any real-world action.

02 / MISSION

Prove a narrow job first.

Start with permissioned accounting, bank, receivables and payroll CSVs for two clients. Use manual accountant-reviewed weekly briefs to establish measurable incremental value before considering more integrations or any broader system.

03 / WHAT IS KNOWN

An existing category. An unproven distinction.

The original research brief grades evidence for the existing forecasting category as medium, and evidence for agentic action differentiation as low. These are research judgements, not independent product validation.

  1. Existing substitutes

    Fathom, Jirav, an accountant or fractional CFO, QuickBooks/Xero reports and spreadsheet forecasts already occupy adjacent parts of the workflow. Category overlap does not prove demand or superiority.

  2. Open questions

    Retention across owner cohorts, willingness to pay for reviewed decisions and distribution economics remain unproven.

  3. Expansion hypothesis

    Recurring accountant-mediated advisory could expand through vertical templates and integrations if retention and channel economics are demonstrated. SME counts alone are not evidence.

04 / CURRENT STATUS

Honest about what has not happened.

There are no demonstrated live integrations, real customers, validated demand, accepted prices or revenue claims in this preview. The demo uses invented records only. RunwayMemo is the selected name; domain ownership and trademark clearance are not asserted.

THE NEXT HANDOVER

Bring the owner and accountant.

A bounded pilot would need both people, permissioned records and a purchase decision. This preview takes no payment or sensitive records.
