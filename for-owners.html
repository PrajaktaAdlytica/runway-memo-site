01 / THE QUESTION

Pay now, or wait for a receipt?

In the fictional Elmfield Repairs example, opening cash is $32,000. Payroll is $18,000 and the supplier bill is $12,000. The $15,000 customer receipt is expected after payroll, not before.

  1. Pay supplier before payroll

    Friday cash is $2,000. This illustration assumes no other movements and does not assess whether that balance is sufficient for the real business.

  2. Hold supplier payment

    Friday cash is $14,000. The supplier remains unpaid; obligations and commercial consequences still need human review.

02 / YOUR PART

Bring a decision, not just a report request.

The pilot is intended for established cash-constrained services businesses with a recurring weekly decision and an external accountant or fractional CFO able to supervise it.

  1. Document the baseline

    Describe how the decision is made today, which tools you use and what information is missing.

  2. Review the alternatives

    Question assumptions with the accountant. A scenario is not guaranteed runway advice.

  3. Say what changed

    After each cycle, identify a recommendation that actually changed your action. The accountant corroborates it.

03 / WHEN TO PAUSE

Better presentation cannot fix unreliable books.

If the records cannot be reconciled, the uncertainty must remain visible. If the owner only wants free reports or the accountant must rebuild every input each week, stop or change the pilot scope.

BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Keep the questions open.

What if the books do not reconcile?+

The discrepancy needs accountant review before a recommendation can be relied on. If the accountant has to rebuild every input each week, that is a reason to stop or change the pilot.

Who needs to join the pilot?+

The business owner and the supervising external accountant or fractional CFO. They agree the scope and co-sign a paid pilot. Accountant and bookkeeper co-delivery is a proposed channel; no partnership is claimed.

Is RunwayMemo available to buy?+

This is a concept preview, not an operating product. Modules and pricing are hypotheses. The request form demonstrates a consented request; it does not enrol you, send your details or collect payment.

THE NEXT HANDOVER

A bounded pilot would need both people, permissioned records and a purchase decision. This preview takes no payment or sensitive records.
