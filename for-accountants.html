01 / THE REVIEWER’S JOB

Reconcile, challenge, amend.

Start with the owner’s specific decision. Inspect the permissioned source packet, identify omissions, challenge receipt timing and record corrections before the owner reviews the memo.

  1. Source reconciliation

    Compare books and bank evidence. Record missing or contradictory inputs rather than treating imported data as reliable.

  2. Reviewable assumptions

    Dates, held bills and uncertain receipts stay explicit. An amendment changes the scenario, not the source record.

  3. Measured review time

    Track review minutes, corrections and omissions every cycle. Time savings are a hypothesis to test, not a claim on this site.

02 / CO-DELIVERY

Build around an existing professional relationship.

Accounting firms are the proposed reseller or co-delivery channel. Bookkeepers and fractional CFOs may help recruit suitable owner–accountant pairs. No existing partnership or reseller programme is claimed.

  1. Agree responsibilities

    Define the reviewer, scope, permissions and owner authority before the pilot. Advisory liability and client expectations need professional review.

  2. Make channel fit explicit

    Do not assume a firm wants another tool or that a reseller margin works. Test willingness to deliver, review effort, channel conflict and willingness to pay.

  3. Start manually

    Have two accountants prepare weekly owner briefs with clients’ permission. Compare the existing process with four reviewed cycles.

03 / THE EVIDENCE GATE

A polished memo is not retention.

The useful evidence is an owner-reported changed decision corroborated by the accountant, reconciled forecast error, review minutes and a paid renewal supported by both parties. Expansion depends on distribution and retention economics.

BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Keep the questions open.

How is this different from forecasting tools?+

RunwayMemo explores a narrow weekly decision memo with source evidence, timing scenarios and an accountant amendment trail. Forecasting software and spreadsheets may already serve parts of that job. Whether reviewed memos add value is a pilot question, not a proven claim.

What data would a pilot use?+

Permissioned accounting, bank, receivables and payroll CSVs for a small, agreed scope. The initial research plan is two clients with their accountants. This preview uses fictional records only; there are no live integrations or file uploads.

Who needs to join the pilot?+

The business owner and the supervising external accountant or fractional CFO. They agree the scope and co-sign a paid pilot. Accountant and bookkeeper co-delivery is a proposed channel; no partnership is claimed.

THE NEXT HANDOVER

Bring the owner and accountant.

A bounded pilot would need both people, permissioned records and a purchase decision. This preview takes no payment or sensitive records.
